# Driver guide

Portal: https://portal.dlvlogistics.com . All times are Eastern (ET).

## Install the app
**iPhone**
1. Open the portal in **Safari** (not another browser).
2. Tap **Share**, then **Add to Home Screen**, then **Add**.

**Android**
1. Open the portal in **Chrome**.
2. Tap the menu (three dots), then **Install app**.

Open the app from your home screen from now on.

## Sign in
1. Enter your email and tap **Send code**.
2. Open the email and type the 8 digit code in the app (or tap **Sign in**). Do not use the email link inside the installed app.
3. No email? Tap **Resend code** after the 30 second countdown.
4. Wrong or expired code: tap **Resend code**.

## Run a load
Open **My loads**, then tap your load. One big button moves the load to the next step. The labels, in order:
1. **Arrived at pickup**
2. **Start loading**
3. **Leave for delivery**
4. **Arrived at delivery**
5. **Mark delivered**

Tap it once per step. It also tells you the "Next status".

## Leaving for delivery (ETA)
When you tap **Leave for delivery**, a box asks for your delivery ETA (Eastern time). It starts at the delivery appointment time. Change it to when you really expect to arrive, then tap **Confirm ETA and leave**. Dispatch and the customer see it.

## Update the ETA
While Enroute or At delivery, tap **Update ETA** next to "Delivery ETA", set the new time, tap **Save ETA**.

## Proof of delivery (POD)
1. Tap **Mark delivered**.
2. If you have the signed paperwork, tap the big box (**Drop the file here, or tap to choose**) and take a clear photo. Check the preview.
3. Tap **Mark delivered**.

The photo is optional. No photo yet? Just tap **Mark delivered**. The load is delivered and you add the photo later.

## Add the POD photo later
Open the delivered load from **My loads**. The **Proof of delivery** card says **POD not uploaded yet** in orange. Tap the box, take the photo, then tap **Add POD photo**. Once a POD is saved the card shows **View POD** and **Add another** (for a second page).

## If the photo fails
You will see "The photo did not upload" with two buttons.
1. Check your signal, then tap **Try again**.
2. No signal? Tap **Mark delivered without photo**. The load is delivered and you add the photo later from the load page.
3. Still failing? Call dispatch.

## Call and map
Each stop shows the contact. Tap the big **Call** button to phone them. **Open in Maps** starts directions.

## Carrier owners: Team page
Tap **Team** in the top menu.
- Add: type the driver email (and name), tap **Add driver**. They sign in with a code.
- Remove: tap **Remove** next to the driver, then **Yes, remove**. They lose access right away.
