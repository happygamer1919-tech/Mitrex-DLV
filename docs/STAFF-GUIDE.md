# Dispatch guide for staff (Luca)

Portal: https://portal.dlvlogistics.com . All times are Eastern (ET). This page is for staff only: the ITS load to copy is never shown to Maria or to carriers.

## When Maria requests a load
1. You get an email "New load requested (Request MTX-0005)". It lists the route, truck size, pickup and delivery times, contacts and, near the top, one of two lines:
   - **ITS load to copy: 1269 (Mitrex to 481 University Ave, 26 ft)**. That is the old ITS load to copy for this lane and truck size.
   - **No ITS reference for this lane and size yet.** There is a link to add it (see the last section).
2. Tap the link in the email, or open **Board** and tap the load. The load page opens with a big box at the top, **ITS load to copy**, showing the number. Tap **Copy** to put the number on your clipboard (it says "Copied 1269").

## Create the load in ITS
1. In ITS, find the old load with that number and copy it.
2. Change the dates, times and anything that differs for this request (the portal page has the details).
3. Save the new load. ITS gives it a new load number.

## Back in the portal
1. Type the NEW ITS load number in **ITS load number (required to book)** (digits, a split such as 313-2 is fine).
2. In **Carrier and booking**, pick the carrier and tap **Save carrier**.
3. Upload the BOL when you have it (drop the file or tap the box, then **Upload BOL**). You can book without it; Maria is told it will follow, and is emailed the file when you upload it.
4. Tap **Mark booked**. The button stays grey until the carrier is saved and the ITS number is typed, and it says which one is missing. Maria and the carrier are emailed.
5. Typed the wrong ITS number? **Edit ITS number** on the load fixes it any time.

## Photos of a load (pickup and delivery)
Carriers must take a photo of the loaded freight before they can tap Leave for delivery, and a delivery photo before Mark delivered. Maria sees them on her load page after the status changes.
- Open the load: the **Documents** card lists every photo as **Pickup photo** or **Delivery photo** with the time (Eastern time) and an **Open** link.
- **Stuck load** (a driver with a broken camera, a forgotten photo): use **Override status** to move the load to **Enroute** or **Delivered**. It works without a photo. The timeline records it: "Staff skipped the pickup photo (none on file)." or "Staff skipped the delivery photo (none on file)." after your note.
- **Upload on behalf of the carrier**: in **Documents** use **Pickup photo** or **Delivery photo** (drop or choose a png, jpg or webp, then **Upload Pickup photo** or **Upload Delivery photo**). This also unlocks the driver's button.
- **Remove a photo** taken by mistake: **Remove** next to it. Maximum 6 photos per kind per load.
- A load's photos are deleted with the load when an admin uses Delete forever.

## On the board
Requested loads show a small line "Copy ITS 1269" so you can see the number without opening the load. Loads with no reference show "No ITS reference".

## Compact board and load pages
- Each status is a band you can fold: tap its title (for example "Delivered 214") to open or close it. Delivered and Cancelled start closed, the others open.
- An open band shows its first 12 loads. Tap **Show all** for the rest.
- Delivered and Cancelled hold the pickups of the last 30 days. Tap **Show older (50 more)** to add older loads, 50 at a time.
- Each load is one line: number, route, pickup date and time, carrier (or Not assigned) and the badges (BOL pending, POD pending, Copy ITS, Return). Tap the line to open the load, or tap **Details** for the contacts, size and Moffett, PO, notes and ETA.
- A search or filter looks through every delivered and cancelled load, not only the last 30 days, and opens the bands that have a match.
- One list view shows 50 rows, then **Show more**.
- On a load page the Timeline shows the latest 3 events; tap **Show all ... events** for the rest. The uploads are under **Upload files** (the files themselves stay visible above). **Override status** and **Cancel load** are under **More actions**. Delete forever (admins) is unchanged at the bottom.
- Other lists fold too: on **Locations** tap **Edit** to see the contact, notes, who added it and the edit form; on **Lanes** each pickup is a closed group until you search or filter; on **Rate requests** Quoted and Cancelled are closed; on **Location requests** the reviewed ones; on **Users** the deactivated users.

## Find loads on the board (filters and sorting)
- Tap a quick pill: Today, Tomorrow, Next 7 days, Not assigned, Needs BOL, Needs ITS number. Tap it again to switch it off.
- Tap Filters for search (ITS number, request number, PO, place or carrier), status, carrier, shipper (pickup), receiver (delivery), size and custom dates, then Sort by and Order.
- View "One list" shows every match in one list (a table on a computer, cards on a phone). "By status" keeps the status bands.
- Each active filter shows as a chip. Tap a chip to remove it, or Clear all. While a filter is on, the counts read like "3 of 9".

## Return loads (the truck takes the empty frames back)
- Maria books a return from a load that is At delivery or Delivered. You get an email "New return load requested (Request MTX-0007)" whose first lines say "A new return load was requested by Mitrex." and "Return of 313, original carrier: Kaja Transport" (or "Not assigned"). Everything else in the email is as usual, including the ITS load to copy line.
- On the board (both views) and on the calendar a return load has a small purple **Return** badge next to its number.
- The return load page shows the **Return** badge and "Return of 313" with a link to the first load. The first load lists it under "Return loads".
- While a return has no carrier, **Carrier and booking** shows "Original carrier: <name>" and a **Use the same carrier** button. It saves that carrier exactly like **Save carrier** (same emails: nothing is sent while the load is Requested; the carrier is emailed when you book). Then enter the ITS number and **Mark booked** as usual.
- A return picks up at a site that may not be a normal shipper and may go back to a shipper that is not a normal receiver. That is allowed for returns only.
- You can also make a return yourself: open a load that is At delivery or Delivered and tap **Create return**. The form is filled the other way round (pickup is the site, delivery is the first shipper; choose another place if the frames go elsewhere). Add the dates and tap **Create return**. The return is a Requested load for the same customer, linked to the first load, and no request email goes out. You land on the new load: use **Use the same carrier**, enter the ITS number and book as usual.

## Change a load (any detail, any time before it is cancelled)
- Open the load and tap **Edit details** (top of the page). It works at every status except Cancelled, also after the load is booked, moving or delivered.
- You can change the pickup place, the delivery place, both contacts, both times (appointment or window, dates and hours), the truck size, Moffett, weight, pieces, PO number and notes. The status, the carrier and the ITS number keep their own controls on the load page.
- Past dates are allowed for staff (a load that is moving or delivered has dates in the past). The delivery still cannot be before the pickup, and a time window must end after it starts.
- The lists offer every active place that can ship (pickup) or receive (delivery), plus the places the load has now even if they were switched off since. If you change one place, the other one must still be usable; the page tells you if it is not.
- **Email the updated details to the carrier**: tick it to send the carrier one email "Load 313 updated" with what changed and all the current details. Leave it unticked (the default) to save quietly. After saving the page says "Saved.", "Saved. The carrier was emailed." or "Saved. The carrier email could not be sent." (the change is saved in every case; call the carrier if the email failed).
- Every change is written in the timeline as "Details edited: ..." with the old and the new value, under your name. Maria and the carrier see that line in their timelines, and their pages show the new details straight away. Maria is not emailed.
- To send the carrier the details again without changing anything, use **Email the details to the carrier again** in **Carrier and booking** (any booked, moving or delivered load with a carrier).

## When there is no reference for a lane
The load page shows an amber box "No ITS reference for this lane and size". Use the old way for this one (look the lane up in ITS yourself), then tap **Add it** in the box. The Lanes page opens with the pickup, delivery and size filled in. Type the ITS load you used and tap **Add lane**. The next request for the same lane and size shows it. The same row serves every load of that lane and size, so one entry is enough.

## Fix or change a number
Open **Lanes** in the top menu. Tap **Edit** on the row, change the number (or the pickup, the delivery or the truck size if the row was entered for the wrong lane), tap **Save**. If another row already has that pickup, delivery and size, the page says so and nothing changes: edit that row instead. To change many rows at once use **Export CSV**, edit the file and **Import CSV** (the page tells you which rows are wrong; nothing is imported until every row is right).

## Rate requests (Maria asks for a price on a new lane)
This is separate from booking. A rate request is not a load and creates no load.
1. The admin gets an email "Rate request RQ-0001: Toronto, ON to Buffalo, NY, 53 ft" with the lane, equipment, weight, dimensions, notes and a link. (Only active admins get it; CSR staff see the same requests under **Rate requests** in the top menu, and the board shows "Open rate requests" with a count.)
2. Open **Rate requests** (top menu). **Open** requests are listed first, then **Quoted**, then **Cancelled**. Tap a request.
3. Check the details, then under **Enter the rate** type the amount (numbers, up to two decimals), choose **CAD** (default) or **USD**, add notes for Maria if you want, and a **Valid until** date if the price expires. Tap **Save rate**. Admin and CSR can both do this.
4. The page says "Rate saved. The customer was emailed that the rate is in the app." Maria gets an email that says the rate is ready; the email never shows the price. She sees the amount on her **Rates** page. If the page says the email could not be sent, tell Maria by phone that the rate is in the app.
5. Entered the wrong amount? Open the request again: **Current rate** is shown and the form is filled in. Change it and tap **Correct the rate**. Maria gets a short email "Your rate was updated in the app" (still no price).
6. If Maria cancelled the request the page says so and the form is gone.

## Locations Maria adds or changes herself
Maria adds new locations and changes every detail of a location herself (name, address, ship, receive, Moffett, default contact, notes). There is no request to approve and no email: her change is live at once, also in the booking form.
- On **Locations** (top menu) each location shows "Added by <name>, <date> ET" (for locations added since this change) and "Last edited by <name>, <date> ET".
- Her new locations are always active and never flagged for review. Only staff can switch a location off, flag it for review or delete it.
- A changed address shows on every load that uses the location, also on booked loads. If she changes a place used by a load on the road, check the carrier has the right address.
- **Requests** (top menu) still lists location requests she sent before this change; approve or reject them as before. Before you approve an old address change, open the location: if Maria already changed it herself, approving would put the older values from the request back, so reject the request instead.
