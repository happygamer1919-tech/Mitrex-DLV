# Spec

Source of truth for WHAT the DLV portal does. Requirements are numbered R1..Rn. `docs/SPEC-COVERAGE.md` maps each R-number to evidence. Decisions that refine this spec are in DECISIONS.md.

DLV Logistics is a freight brokerage (Canada/US). Client Mitrex (also Cladify) ships flatbed loads, mostly from Etobicoke to construction sites in the GTA and Ontario. Their rep Maria books by email today. The portal is "DLV" at https://portal.dlvlogistics.com (Vercel project mitrex-dlv).

## Platform
- R1 Next.js (App Router, TypeScript), Tailwind, Supabase (Postgres, Auth, Storage, Realtime, RLS). Installable PWA: manifest, icons (192, 512, maskable), minimal service worker, standalone display, theme color #020814. No push.
- R2 All times display in America/Toronto and are labelled "ET".
- R3 Invite-only login, no passwords, public signup off. Login is by emailed code (8 digits on hosted Supabase) with the emailed link as fallback; the code works from the installed iPhone PWA.
- R4 Roles: staff_admin, staff_csr, customer, carrier_owner, carrier_driver, stored in profiles. staff_admin and staff_csr have identical operational rights. Only staff_admin manages users, carriers and customers.
- R5 Customers are modelled (customers table, profiles.customer_id). Customer Mitrex is seeded. Maria is its only user.
- R6 One carrier_owner login per carrier company. The owner adds and removes drivers by email (carrier_driver, same carrier). Drivers see exactly what the carrier sees.
- R7 scripts/seed-users.mjs creates the invited users and carriers from SEED_* env names, idempotently, and refuses non-local targets without an explicit flag.
- R8 Every table has RLS on. All privileges are revoked from PUBLIC and anon, then granted narrowly to authenticated.

## Data and rules
- R9 locations table and the 19 seeded locations (supabase/seed.sql, idempotent). SAMIH and the seven sites 1HAM, 152 Sh, 831 Queen, Glengarry, Kitney site, Military Trailsite and PrimeFab require Moffett (DLV-028, migration 0016; the booking form locks the Moffett toggle on for them). Scion Powder Coatings is flagged needs_review.
- R10 location_requests (kind new or change, payload, status pending, approved or rejected).
- R11 loads carry the specified columns and constraints: load_number MTX-0001 style (the internal REQUEST REF, generated when Maria requests the load, unchanged and unique) and its_load_number (the ITS load number, DLV-025: null until staff enter it, digits with an optional dash and digits such as 313-2, unique where set, changed only through set_its_load_number), equipment 26/36/53 (48 ft was removed in DLV-021; 36 stays for upcoming projects), appointment or window timing for pickup and delivery, per-load contact snapshots, status enum, carrier, ETA and timestamps.
- R12 load_events is an append-only audit of every status change and of every ITS load number set or correction (a row with from_status equal to to_status and the note "ITS load number set to X" or "changed from X to Y").
- R13 load_documents (bol, pod) in a private bucket "documents", path {load_id}/{kind}/{uuid}.{ext}, with storage RLS mirroring load access. Customers read BOL and POD of own loads, carriers read BOL and upload POD for own loads (while enroute, at delivery or delivered, never a BOL), staff do everything (staff may upload a POD on behalf of the carrier), only staff upload BOL. Uploads use the shared DropZone control (drop a file, tap, or press Enter or Space on it; 15 MB limit; accepted extensions checked in the browser and by the bucket).
- R14 Status flow runs only through set_load_status: requested, booked (staff, needs a carrier AND the ITS load number: "enter the ITS load number before booking"; the same rule applies to a staff override jump out of requested, cancelling needs none, and legacy loads already booked without a number move forward normally), at_pickup, loading, enroute (needs ETA), at_delivery, delivered (a POD is NOT required: it is optional at delivery and may be uploaded afterwards, DLV-021). Carriers move one step forward at a time. Staff can set any status with a note.
- R15 Cancel: the customer only while requested; staff any time before delivered.
- R16 The customer may edit own load only while requested. A trigger blocks non-staff changes to status, carrier_id, eta, load_number, customer_id, and every direct change of its_load_number (it changes only through set_its_load_number, staff only; a customer insert always stores NULL). From booked through at_delivery the load is read-only with the message "Contact DLV to change this load". Delivered shows "Delivered" with the delivered date; cancelled shows "Cancelled".
- R17 A carrier owner or driver can update ETA only while enroute or at_delivery, through set_load_eta.
- R18 INSERT ... RETURNING for a customer works only for their own customer_id (SR-60).
- R19 Live status: Supabase Realtime on loads with a 15 second polling fallback.

## Screens
- R20 /book: pickup (can_ship) and delivery (can_receive) selects, contacts auto-filled from location defaults and editable per load with "save as default for this location", equipment chips, Moffett toggle (auto on and locked if a selected location requires it), appointment or time window independently for pickup and delivery, optional weight, pieces, PO number, notes, and at the end (above the submit button) "How many trucks?" (stepper and numeric field, default 1, whole number 1 to 10; new booking only, never on the edit form). Validation: contacts required, pickup and delivery differ, delivery not before pickup, trucks a whole number from 1 to 10 (also enforced on the server). Request again: /book?from=<load id> opens the form with the pickup and delivery locations, contacts, equipment size, Moffett, weight, pieces, PO number and notes (a first line "Truck i of N" is removed) copied from one of the customer's own DELIVERED loads, the appointment or window choice kept, and every date and time empty; a dismissible notice says "Copied from MTX-nnnn. Choose the new dates and times."; trucks start at 1. A malformed, unknown, foreign or not delivered id silently opens the empty form. Last contact hint: whenever a location is selected, under each contact block "Last contact at <location>: <name>, <phone> (MTX-nnnn, <date ET>)" from the customer's most recent load (created_at, last 200 loads) with that pickup (or delivery) location, with a "Use last contact" button, or "Same as last time" when the fields already match; with no earlier load and a saved default contact the hint reads "No earlier load here. Using the saved default contact."
- R21 Submitting creates one requested load per truck (N loads from one insert, identical details, own request ref and status each (no ITS number yet); notes start "Truck i of N" when N is above 1) and sends ONE email to all active staff listing every request ref with its admin link, subject "New load requested (Request MTX-0005)" and the line "ITS load number: not assigned yet. Enter it when you book." and the "ITS load to copy" line of R39 N=1 redirects to the load, N above 1 to /loads with a banner listing the N request refs. At most 10 trucks per booking and 30 loads per customer user per 10 minutes.
- R22 Number display (DLV-025, one helper src/lib/load-number.ts): the headline is the ITS number once set; before that Maria sees "Number pending" with the request ref as small muted text ("Request MTX-0005"), staff see "Request MTX-0005", carriers only see booked loads so always the ITS number (a legacy booked load without one shows its request ref). /loads: list with status filter and a "Completed" quick filter (delivered loads, newest delivery first, with the delivered date), live; every delivered row has "Request again" (cancelled loads do not). /loads/[id]: timeline from load_events, carrier name once booked, ETA when set, BOL download, POD view, edit and cancel while requested; a delivered load shows a prominent "Request again" button next to the Delivered note.
- R23 /locations: list, edit default contact name and phone, request a new location, request an address change (location_requests pending).
- R24 Staff /admin board (one band per status, stacked top to bottom, load cards in a grid; single column on a phone) and calendar (week and month by pickup date).
- R25 Staff load detail: assign carrier, upload BOL, enter the ITS load number (required field "ITS load number (required to book)"), mark booked, override status with note (asks for the ITS number when leaving requested), cancel, and correct the ITS number afterwards with "Edit ITS number". "Mark booked" stays disabled until a carrier is saved AND an ITS number is entered, and names what is missing. A BOL is NOT required to book: the "BOL pending" badge on booked loads without BOL stays (owner default, see QUESTIONS.md). Booking sends the carrier email and ONE booking confirmation email to the customer (R31).
- R26 Staff: locations manager (CRUD, needs_review), approve or reject location_requests (approve applies the payload), carriers manager, users invite and deactivate (staff_admin only).
- R27 CSV export with date range and status filter. The column load_number holds the ITS number when set, otherwise the request ref; request_ref (always the MTX-0005 style value) follows it.

CSV_COLUMNS: load_number, request_ref, created_at, pickup_location, delivery_location, equipment_size, moffett, weight_lbs, pieces, po_number, pickup_timing, pickup_date, pickup_time_start, pickup_time_end, delivery_timing, delivery_date, delivery_time_start, delivery_time_end, carrier, status, eta, delivered_at

- R28 Carrier /my-loads: cards for loads of own carrier (booked and later).
- R29 Carrier load detail: addresses, contacts with tap-to-call, timing, ONE big next-step button only. Enroute asks for a required ETA; Delivered works with or without a POD photo: the modal says "Add the signed POD photo now if you have it. You can add it later from this load." A chosen photo (camera capture, compressed client side to max 1600px JPEG before upload) that fails to upload shows the error with "Try again" and "Mark delivered without photo" (never delivered silently). After Delivered the load page shows a "Proof of delivery" card: with no POD an amber "POD not uploaded yet" notice and an "Add POD photo" drop area, with a POD a "View POD" link and "Add another". Staff see a "POD pending" badge on delivered loads without a POD (board card and load page); the customer's Documents card says "POD not uploaded yet." until one exists. ETA editable while enroute.
- R30 /team (carrier_owner only): add and remove drivers by email.
- R31 Notifications by email: all staff when a load is requested (request ref, ITS number not assigned yet); carrier users when a load is booked or the carrier is changed on a live load (subject "Load <ITS number> assigned to you"); ALL active customer users of the load's customer get ONE booking confirmation when staff mark the load booked (subject "Load <ITS number> booked": carrier, pickup and delivery names and addresses, times in ET, contacts, equipment, a link to /loads/{id}, the BOL attached as BOL-<ITS number>.<ext>; over 20 MB combined: no attachment and "The BOL is available in the app" with the link; no BOL yet: "The BOL will follow once uploaded"). A BOL uploaded after booking (status not requested) sends "BOL for load <ITS number>" with the file to the same users, same 20 MB rule, nothing while the load is still requested. This deliberately reverses the old "no status emails to Maria" rule for booking only: at pickup, enroute and delivered stay in the app, no email. No SMS, no push. A failing mail service never blocks booking.

## Design and rules
- R32 Plus Jakarta Sans 400/500/700. Pill buttons, card radius 16px, input radius 12px, touch targets at least 44px (48px on driver screens). Colors: ink #020814, neon #2BFF88, mint #E8FBF0, amber #F28C28, card #FFFFFF, border #CFE6D9, muted #4E5F57. Customer and staff pages on mint, driver pages on ink. Status chip colors as specified; Delivered green stays distinct from the mint background and the neon accent.
- R33 Header shows the wordmark "DLV", a neon dot and "Mitrex shipping portal".
- R34 No em or en dashes anywhere in the repository. English only.
- R35 No secrets, .env files or credentials are committed.
- R36 Admin can deactivate and reactivate users; a deactivated user is denied at the database and cannot sign in.
- R37 Deliverables: supabase/migrations, supabase/seed.sql, supabase/tests/rls.sql, scripts/seed-users.mjs, scripts/check-no-dashes.mjs, .env.example (names only), public/manifest.webmanifest and icons, README.
- R38 Admin only permanent delete of a load (DLV-027). Only an ACTIVE staff_admin (chris@dlvlogistics.com) can delete a load forever; staff_csr, customers and carriers never can, in the screen, in the server action and in the database. /admin/loads/[id] shows a "Danger zone" card at the bottom, rendered only for staff_admin (not at all for a csr), with a "Delete this load forever" button that opens an accessible modal: it warns "This permanently deletes the load, its timeline, and its BOL and POD files. This cannot be undone.", shows what will be deleted (load number, route, status, number of files), and asks the admin to type the load number (the ITS number when set, otherwise the request ref). "Delete forever" stays disabled until the text matches exactly; Cancel and Escape close it; focus is trapped inside; targets are at least 44px and it fits 375px. The database function delete_load_forever(load, typed text) checks the role and the text again, locks the load, writes a minimal audit row (table load_deletions: request ref, ITS number, last status, who, when, and any files that could not be removed), deletes the load and, by cascade, its timeline events and document rows, and returns the storage paths; the app then removes those files from the private documents bucket and redirects to /admin with the banner "Load <number> deleted forever". If a file cannot be removed the load stays deleted, the paths are logged in the audit row (record_load_deletion_orphans) and the banner says "The load was deleted. N files could not be removed and were logged for cleanup." There is no general DELETE privilege or policy on loads: the function is the only path. A double click deletes once. Lists, board, calendar and CSV no longer contain the load (live refresh), and its pages are not found. Deleting one truck of a multi-truck booking leaves the other trucks. It cannot be undone: only the weekly backup can bring a load back.
- R39 Lane references, STAFF ONLY (DLV-028). DLV dispatches in ITS: for each repeat lane the staff copy an old ITS load, and the app says which one. A scenario is pickup location + delivery location + truck size (26, 36 or 53); table lane_references holds one row per scenario (UNIQUE), the ITS load to copy (digits with an optional dash and digits, at most 30 characters, same shape as an ITS load number), an optional note, updated_at and updated_by (from the session). The owner's 25 lanes are seeded by migration 0016 (joined to locations by name, all or nothing, idempotent); the lanes the owner left out on purpose are not invented. The Moffett column of the owner's table is DERIVED (pickup or delivery requires Moffett), never stored. Access: only an active staff_admin or staff_csr can read, insert, update or delete (four RLS policies gated by dlv_is_staff(), identical rights); customers, carrier owners, carrier drivers, inactive staff and anon get nothing, and the table is not in the realtime publication. The reference is evaluated live from the load's current pickup, delivery and size (no snapshot). Where it shows, and only there: (1) /admin/lanes (nav item "Lanes" for both staff roles): all lanes grouped by pickup (pickup, delivery, size, ITS load to copy, derived Moffett, last updated by and when), search and filters by pickup, delivery and size, inline edit of number and note, delete with confirm, an Add lane form that accepts the query params pickup, delivery and size, a duplicate scenario refused with a clear message, CSV export (columns shipper, receiver, truck_size, load_to_copy, note; cells starting with = + - @ are protected against formula injection) and CSV import (paste or upload through the DropZone, a preview that validates every row: unknown location names, bad size, bad number, duplicates inside the file, pickup equal to delivery, with a message per row; Apply imports only when the whole file is valid, at most 500 rows, as ONE upsert on the scenario key, and the server validates again); (2) /admin/loads/[id]: a prominent "ITS load to copy" card above the booking card with the number large, a Copy button (clipboard API with a fallback, announced to screen readers) and the scenario text ("Mitrex to 481 University Ave, 26 ft"), or the amber "No ITS reference for this lane and size" with an "Add it" link to /admin/lanes prefilled; (3) the admin board: a small "Copy ITS <number>" line on Requested loads only; (4) the staff request email (R21): "ITS load to copy: <number> (<pickup> to <delivery>, <size> ft)" or "No ITS reference for this lane and size yet. Add it: <link>", one line per distinct scenario, read with the service role only inside that staff only path after the load is created. The number never appears in the booking confirmation, the carrier assignment email, any customer or carrier page, the loads CSV export, or any realtime payload.
- R40 Rate requests (DLV-030). A customer (Maria) sometimes needs a RATE for a new lane that is not in the system. She submits a rate request on /rates (customers only, menu item "Rates"): pickup city and state, delivery city and state (state = two letter US state, DC or Canadian province code, stored upper case), equipment 26 / 36 / 53 ft, optional weight (whole lbs, 1 to 100000), optional dimensions (at most 200 characters), optional notes (at most 1000). A rate request is NOT a load: it books nothing and creates no load. Table rate_requests (migration 0017): ref RQ-0001 style from its own sequence, customer_id, requested_by (always the signed in user, set by a trigger), the request columns, status open / quoted / cancelled, and the quote (quoted_amount > 0, quoted_currency CAD or USD, quote_notes, quote_valid_until, quoted_by, quoted_at); a CHECK keeps the four quote fields set exactly when status is quoted. RLS: a customer reads and inserts only rows of its own customer_id (the insert grant is column level and names only the nine request columns, so status, quote fields, ref and requested_by can never be written by a client); active staff read all; carriers read nothing; NO update or delete grant or policy for anyone. Only two SECURITY DEFINER functions change a row: set_rate_quote (active staff_admin or staff_csr; stamps quoted_by and quoted_at; refuses a cancelled request; an already quoted request may be re-quoted to correct the rate) and cancel_rate_request (a user of the request's customer, open requests only). The table is not in the realtime publication; the pages poll every 15 seconds. Customer page /rates: the request form (server validation, 30 requests per 10 minutes per user), the customer's requests newest first with a badge (Waiting for rate, Rate ready, Cancelled), and for a quoted request the amount with currency, notes, valid until and a plain "Book this lane" link to /book; an open request can be cancelled. Staff pages (menu item "Rate requests"): /admin/rates (open first, then quoted, then cancelled, with counts; the board shows the open count) and /admin/rates/[id] (details, the form that enters the rate: amount, currency CAD default or USD, notes, optional valid until; the existing rate can be corrected). Emails (plain text, Resend, RESEND_BASE_URL honoured, a failure never fails the action): on creation ONE email to every ACTIVE staff_admin (not csr, not inactive users, not customers) with subject "Rate request RQ-0001: <pickup city, ST> to <delivery city, ST>, <size> ft", the lane, equipment, weight, dimensions, notes, customer name and a link to /admin/rates/<id>; when staff enter the rate ONE email to every ACTIVE customer user of that customer, subject "Your rate is ready in the app (RQ-0001)", with a link to /rates and NO amount, currency or quote notes (the rate lives in the app only); a correction sends "Your rate was updated in the app (RQ-0001)" the same way.
- R41 Load photos (DLV-032). The carrier must photograph the freight at two steps and the app takes the picture itself. (1) On the step loading to enroute (button "Leave for delivery") the carrier first takes at least one "Loaded photo" (kind pickup_photo): while the load is loading the big button is a neon "Take loaded photo" step and "Leave for delivery" stays disabled until a photo is stored. (2) On the step at_delivery to delivered (button "Mark delivered") the carrier first takes at least one "Delivery photo" (kind delivery_photo), the same way ("Take delivery photo"). No status was added: the owner's "Unloading" maps to at_delivery (docs/QUESTIONS.md). The gate is in the database: set_load_status refuses a carrier_owner or carrier_driver loading to enroute without a pickup_photo row and at_delivery to delivered without a delivery_photo row, with a plain message; staff_admin and staff_csr are exempt (dispatch can fix a stuck load) and when staff move past a missing photo the load_events note says "Staff skipped the pickup photo (none on file)." or "Staff skipped the delivery photo (none on file)." (appended to their own note). The rest of set_load_status is unchanged. The pictures are taken INSIDE the app: src/components/camera/CameraCapture.tsx opens the rear camera with getUserMedia (facingMode environment), shows a live preview and a big Take photo button, then Use this photo or Retake, downscales on a canvas to at most 1600 px on the long side as JPEG quality 0.8, and uploads. The in-app camera is the primary button. Besides it, a secondary button "Choose a photo from your phone" (input type=file accept image/jpeg, image/png, image/webp, no capture attribute, so iOS converts HEIC to JPEG and the gallery or files open) is offered on the loaded photo and delivery photo steps; a chosen picture goes through the same 1600 px JPEG downscale and the same Use this photo or Retake review, at least one photo from either source unlocks the next step, the database rules of 0018 are unchanged (no migration) and the official time stays the server time of the upload (an old gallery picture shows the upload time, never its own date). If the camera cannot be opened (permission denied, no camera, no API) the component says so in plain words and offers one fallback, an input type=file accept=image/* capture=environment that opens the phone's camera app. The camera stream is stopped after the capture and on unmount. 44px or larger buttons, a live status message, works at 375px. The same component is the primary way to take the POD (a small "Choose a file instead" link remains for a PDF the receiver handed over); the POD stays OPTIONAL and is not a gate. The official time of a photo is the server time of the insert (load_documents.created_at, shown in Eastern time, "ET"); the browser may send captured_at, stored in its own nullable column and never trusted or shown. Storage: load_documents.kind and the strict anchored path shape allow pickup_photo and delivery_photo (image files only: jpg, jpeg, png, webp); at most 6 photos per kind per load (database trigger); a photo row can only be inserted when its image file is already in the bucket and the object is declared as an image. dlv_can_access_doc: staff read and write everything; a carrier owner or driver of the load's own carrier WRITES a pickup_photo only while the load is at_pickup or loading and a delivery_photo only while it is at_delivery; the customer READS (never writes) a pickup_photo only once the load is enroute, at_delivery or delivered and a delivery_photo only once it is delivered, never of another customer's load; BOL and POD rules are unchanged. A carrier can remove a photo it took itself while the step is still open, and staff can remove any photo, only through delete_load_photo (there is no DELETE privilege on load_documents). Maria's load page /loads/[id] has a Photos card (kind label, Eastern server time, tap to open full size through a short lived signed link); nothing before the status allows it (the database is the control, the page only shows what comes back). The staff load page lists every photo with its time, can upload and remove photos on behalf of the carrier, and a staff status override to enroute or delivered works without photos. Deleting a load forever removes the photo files too. Manual gate M12: the camera and its permission prompt cannot be verified by automation (docs/ACCEPTANCE-CHECKLIST.md).
